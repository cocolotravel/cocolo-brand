COCOLO Travel builds bespoke journeys in Japan for curious travelers. The
positioning is **Travel Artisans**: every detail considered, every transfer
and room and guide chosen with intention, so that when something unexpected
happens — and it will — the traveler is free to follow it. That is not a flaw
in the plan. That is the plan.

## The four pillars

Everything — copy, design, photography, product — is measured against these
four. They're not moods; each is a concrete rule for the work.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Kodawari" icon="seti:config">
    The pursuit of excellence in every detail. Nothing left to chance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sensorial" icon="star">
    Speak to the senses, the heart, the inner child. Wonder and discovery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stillness" icon="moon">
    Calm, confident, well-informed — the tone of a quality magazine.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wabi-Sabi" icon="pencil">
    The beauty of imperfection, the magic of unexpected discovery.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
