Type carries the brand's voice. A characterful display face for headings and
moments of personality, a calm humanist sans for everything you actually read.
The specimens and scale below are rendered live from the tokens.

<TypeScale />

## Using type

Lead with the **display** face for headings and the occasional oversized phrase —
it's where the brand's warmth shows. Keep body copy in the **body** face at a
comfortable reading size, and resist setting long passages in the display face.

The scale is a system, not a buffet: pick the step that fits the hierarchy rather
than nudging arbitrary pixel values. Consistent steps are what make a set of pages
feel like one brand.
