## Using color

The cloud neutrals carry almost everything — backgrounds, text, surfaces, and
dividers. Reach for **sky** as the primary interactive color (links, buttons,
focus) and keep the warm **accents** rare: a small amount of dawn or gold is a
highlight, not a background.

A simple test: if a page feels busy, the fix is usually less accent, not more
neutral. The brand should feel like open sky, not a crowded one.

:::caution[Contrast]
Light cloud tones on white fail accessibility contrast for body text. Use
**storm** or **ink** for text on light backgrounds, and reserve the pale tones
for surfaces and large display elements. When in doubt, check the pairing against
WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text).
:::
