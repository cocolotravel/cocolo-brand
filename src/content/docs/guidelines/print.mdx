Roadbooks and proposals are sometimes printed, on **A4**. The same named
document styles switch from screen sizes to point sizes under
`@media print`, read from the token file's print override block.

## A4 rules

- **White background, no shadows.** Printers don't render shadows or
  translucent fills reliably — flatten to a solid white page.
- **Navigation and buttons hidden.** Tab bars, nav chrome, and any
  interactive-only control have no role on paper.
- **All accordions open.** Nothing collapsed should stay collapsed —
  a reader can't tap to expand a printed page.
- **Cards get a thin border instead of a fill.** Pale background fills can
  drift grey or pink on some printers; a border reads reliably in black and
  white.
- **No page break inside a table or a callout.** Keep each one on a single
  page so a reader never has to flip to see the rest of a row.
- **Headings stay with the paragraph that follows.** A heading alone at the
  bottom of a page is an orphan — keep it with its content.
- **External link addresses printed in full.** A reader on paper can't tap a
  link, so the URL itself needs to be visible.
